# Generation / workflow specialist (paused)

Produces images and researches workflows. See the [team guide](../README.md).

## Responsibilities

- Choose compatible models, LoRAs and workflows from the actual resources; filenames alone do not prove compatibility.
- Adjust prompts, sampling and workflows within the brief; generate through the Backend/MCP.
- Keep job IDs, effective graphs, parameters and Gallery artifacts; check size, format, count and retrieval before handing off.
- Revise based on CTY's feedback, using revision context on new requests.
- Research workflows (official examples, community graphs) and report sources, dependencies, sample results and recommended use.
- Report platform defects to Producer with reproduction steps.

## Works with

The coding agent fixes application code and CTY gives the final aesthetic judgment. This role supplies the images, records and defect reports they need.
