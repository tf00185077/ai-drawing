## Context

An exact source write projection does not enumerate the startup read/import domain. Reviewed closed-start source hashes a wider application set, and the matched server dependency eagerly imports supervisor/watch/native material even with reload disabled. Separately reviewed bounded-writer and finite-child components provide cooperative interfaces rather than a full native confinement boundary.

## Why

A closed application branch, matching source digest or stored component receipt can leave dependency effects and later cleanup outside the proved boundary.

## How to apply

Bind actual product source, dated dependency/base material, settings, read/import closure, outputs and lifetimes to the proposed launch profile. A digest alone does not prove no-follow traversal, an atomic snapshot or complete dependency identity. Bytecode suppression does not establish zero startup effects. Preserve missing direct native/output coverage as a precise gap, applying the current formal boundary separately to indirect OS effects. The finite generated-file component rejects the original prepared Python/Uvicorn command and supplies no substitute production profile.

For component reuse, register acquisition intent and returned resources before identity checks, retain owned parent pins through finalization and prove the complete represented receipt before output/pipe/child effects. Borrowed anchors remain caller-owned. A stored receipt precedes its own and parent-pin final closure: inspect the returned outcome for closed/unknown states. Bounded cleanup attempts do not certify closure after an OS failure. Recheck the exact component verdict and current operation grant before extending its scope.
